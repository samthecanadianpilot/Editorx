# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Professional video editors, content creators, and social media managers looking for an advanced, highly tactical web-based video editing experience.

## Product Purpose

EditorX brings professional-grade video editing features like dynamic masking and custom effect stacking to the browser, offering a fluid and precise alternative to heavy desktop applications.

## Brand Personality

Precise, technical, stealthy, expert, tactile.

## Anti-references

Generic SaaS dashboards with identical card grids and side-stripe alerts.
Consumer "toy" apps with overly vibrant, uncoordinated colors.
Default "dark mode" using pure black `#000` and pure white `#fff` without a cohesive color strategy.

## Design Principles

- **Focus on the canvas:** The viewer is the hero. UI should recede.
- **Expert confidence:** Avoid excessive borders and bounding boxes. Use alignment and typography for structure.
- **Tactile feedback:** Interactions should feel responsive, utilizing subtle, curve-eased motion without bounce.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Ensure high contrast for text. Avoid relying solely on color to convey state (e.g., use checkmarks and clear labels). Keep hover states distinct.
